# The Practice Holder

*The Practice Holder Formation Pathway describes how holders are formed and recognized. This document describes the role itself.*

## Who Practice Holders are

Practice Holders are practitioners the network recognizes as reliable holders of the practice: people whose capacity for presence has matured to the point that they can reliably hold dyadic unfolding for others and help sustain the quality of practice across the network. The network trusts them to do the practice well, and to help others come to do it well.

They are the load-bearing role that makes growth without dilution possible. As the body of Practice Holders matures, more of the network's practice can be held by people other than the Source.

## The work

**Practice alongside members.** Holders remain inside the ordinary rotation, raising the quality of the field by their presence in it.

**Receive and accompany.** Holders help receive newer practitioners and accompany them as the practice takes root — in the Entryway, in Foundations, and in the early seasons of membership.

**Hold where more is needed.** When an encounter, a person, or a moment asks for greater capacity, holders are the ones who can be asked.

**Contribute to formation.** Holders may contribute to learning and formation as the network develops its capacity to train from within the field.

## The role's center

The role exists to hold the practice and sustain its quality; its authority begins and ends in the practice container. Being a Practice Holder is not a rank, and the trust that attaches to the role attaches to the quality of holding — renewed continuously by how the holding is done, not conferred once and kept.

One line is absolute: holders do not pursue or accept sexual or romantic involvement with those in their care.

## Recognition

The ARC is now forming its first Practice Holders. Formation happens through sustained practice, apprenticeship, real holding responsibility, and demonstrated capacity — the pathway is described in *The Practice Holder Formation Pathway*, and the concrete forms recognition will take remain among the network's open questions, held with the practitioners maturing into the role.

# The Source

*The Source Agreement holds the personal covenant. This document describes the role architecturally.*

## What the role is

The ARC has a human Source. In this phase, that is Daniel Thorson.

Following Peter Koenig's Source framework, as articulated by Tom Nixon, the Source is the person who took the first vulnerable step to bring the initiative into being, and who carries creative authority for its direction and integrity. This is the authority of authorship, not rank. It concerns the ARC as an initiative — what belongs within it, how its structures develop, whether they remain true to its ground. It does not extend over another person's interior, spiritual truth, relationships, or life.

The Source is not above the network. The Source is the member who carries this particular role, inside the practice and the agreements like every member. The role can be passed, in time, through conscious succession.

## The work

**Hold the direction.** The Source carries the ARC's orienting horizon — a planet on which anyone can receive attuned loving presence whenever they need it — and discerns when the network's development is tracking toward or away from it.

**Tend the field.** The integrity and relational health of the network are the Source's continuous care: not managed from outside, but tended from within relationship.

**Steward the Logos Text.** Editorial authority over the Text's integrity, held with the commitment to keep it alive — revisable, offered as ways of looking rather than final claims, never ossified into doctrine.

**Teach from inside the practice.** The Source teaches from and remains inside dyadic unfolding, practicing alongside members. Teaching here is not dispensing knowledge but modeling and evoking the practice.

**Participate in discernment.** The Source participates in the ARC's side of membership discernment, and holds final authority over membership in consultation with appropriate accountability bodies, exercised in service of the field's integrity for all members (per the Member Agreement).

**Form the practitioners.** The Source helps form the Practice Holders through whom the field can grow — and through whom the Source's own holding becomes progressively less necessary.

**Honor the growth thresholds.** The network does not scale past a phase boundary without the re-evaluation the design requires. Speed does not become its own driver.

## What the role is not

**Not a guru.** The Source refuses veneration: attributions of supernatural qualities, exceptional consciousness, or unique access to truth are named and redirected, not received.

**Not infallible, and not self-holding.** The Source maintains named relationships of accountability and commits to receiving concerns before defending against them.

**Not permanent.** The role passes through conscious succession, and the Source commits in advance to stepping back, slowing down, or stepping aside when those who hold them discern they cannot hold the field cleanly.

## Accountability

The Source's accountability is layered. Intimate relational accountability — the people closest to the Source, who can see drift before structures can. The Source Agreement — the personal covenant naming specific commitments and consequences, including the income cap and the absolute safeguarding lines. Structural accountability — the emerging awareness function, the visible financial flows, the growth thresholds, and the safeguarding design patterns that keep the Source's authority visible and challengeable; the Member Agreement holds for the Source as it holds for every member. And the practice itself — dyadic unfolding surfaces what is hidden, and the Source is inside it, not above it.

One tension is named rather than hidden: in the founding phase, proximity to the Source is the network's live gradient of access and information — which is itself the classic vector of the cultic pattern (*Safeguarding the Culture*, "The Proximity Problem"). The design's answer is directional: discernment and responsibility diffuse outward as the network matures, on a path a dedicated document is mapping phase by phase. The Source role succeeds by making itself progressively less necessary.

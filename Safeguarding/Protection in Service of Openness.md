# Protection in Service of Openness

*Initial perspective, taxonomy, and design principles for the ARC's information integrity. May 2026 — provisional, sourced by Daniel Thorson.*

**Companion documents:** *Recording in Service of Trust* (upstream principles for recording, voice, and consent), *Anti-Cult Design Patterns*, *The Member Agreement*, *The Source Agreement*.

> **Note.** This document is an initial way of looking. The frame, the taxonomy, the principles — all of it is offered as a working draft to orient the territory and as a site of ongoing unfoldment.

---

## Perspective: why we protect

The ARC's deepest aspiration is to participate in the emergence of an intimate planetary culture — a way of being together where the gap between what we can say in public and what we can say in private is profoundly reduced. The reference is *pre-conquest consciousness*: a state in which it is safe to expose what is true to everyone, and culture is woven of sympathetic attunement and intimate contact. There is no need to hide, because the conditions of safety and trust hold the whole field.

That is not the current state of affairs.

The protection structures we are building inside the ARC are not features of the destination. They are strategic concessions to present-world conditions — bad-faith actors, rivalrous dynamics, the vulnerability of art and practice in a world that does not yet know how to receive them, and the structural patterns by which contemplative communities have historically gone wrong (treated more fully in the *Anti-Cult Design Patterns* document). The protection is not the ARC's commitment; openness is. The protection serves emergence toward the openness that intimate planetary culture would make possible.

Information integrity in the ARC is not a permanent architecture of walls. It is a temporary scaffolding, calibrated to the field's current capacity to hold what is created within it. As the ARC's work succeeds — as trust deepens, as the network's relational fabric strengthens, as the wider world becomes more able to receive the practice — what can be open becomes more open.

The deeper purpose the protection serves is the sanctity, coherence, trust, and safety that make unfolding possible. Safety is not the goal; it is the precondition. The goal is the deepening of the practice, the strengthening of the field, and the robust emergence and fulfillment of the network's shared purpose.

## Design principles

These are foundational principles for the ARC's relationship to its own information integrity. The recording-specific principles in *Recording in Service of Trust* are meant to derive from these.

**Protection serves emergence toward openness.** The ARC's direction is toward an intimate planetary culture in which exposure no longer requires hiding. Every protection is provisional and exists to make safer the conditions under which the field's work can succeed and eventually reach the world. Safety is the precondition; unfolding and shared purpose are the goal.

**Anti-cult design holds authority accountable.** The ARC structurally designs against the concentration of unaccountable authority — through leadership exposure, distribution of records and decision-making outward, refusal of veneration, and the other patterns articulated in the *Anti-Cult Design Patterns* document. The Source may also model transparency through his own freely-chosen vulnerable practice; this is offered as a gift, not a norm imposed on others.

**Consent is the working ground for member-generated material.** For artifacts contributed by members — transcripts, art, communications, contributions to shared intelligence — the contributor's consent is the final word. Consent is revisable. Withdrawing consent is honored.

**Different sensitivities are honored.** What is open for one person needs protection for another. The default within any shared space is set by the more reserved threshold, not the more permissive one.

**Good faith is the working assumption.** Members are received as good-faith participants. Bad faith — willful misuse, weaponization of vulnerable material, extractive translation — is met with firm response, up to and including removal. The good-faith default is what makes the openness possible; the firm response to bad faith is what protects it.

**Relationship and discernment over protocol.** No taxonomy or system replaces the work of sensing what is appropriate in a particular moment, including when to open what has been held. The structures exist to make that discernment easier and more consistent — they do not substitute for it.

## Initial taxonomy of artifact classes

What follows is a first sketch — drawn from somatic sensing rather than analytic completeness. It will need refinement, additions, and likely some collapses, as the work clarifies.

**Logos Text.** Live and public at [publish.obsidian.md/logos-text](https://publish.obsidian.md/logos-text/), on an ongoing basis. Part of how the ARC opens to the world is that the philosophical and practical text is visible — it is part of a prospective member's discernment process.
*Default protection: Public, ongoingly.*

**Design specs and proposals** (the Weekly Weave, the coordination-layer proposals). Currently ask-only. Shared with trusted relations and Foundations Training participants, with a request not to circulate further. At the early phase, visibility serves trust and discernment for prospective members; at a later phase, the specs may become more held within the network as a form of protection. The handling will move with the field's needs.
*Default protection: Holder-Proximate / Source-Intimate now, with conscious release decisions over time.*

**Curricula.** Similar to design specs in their handling — given to those who need them, with derivative public-facing forms (e.g., training landing pages) sourced from them when appropriate.
*Default protection: Member-Open within the ARC, with derivative public forms.*

**Member art.** Some art will need fierce protection — it does not leave the network, and circulation beyond is treated as a violation of the member agreement, with possible removal. This firmness is precisely what makes vulnerable art possible in the first place. The contributor sets the level of protection; the network enforces it.
*Default protection: Source-Intimate by default, contributor-configurable, with firm enforcement on the firm end.*

**Recorded teachings.** Deliberate teaching artifacts — a recorded talk, a teaching released as content — distinct from the byproduct recordings of containers, which are addressed in *Recording in Service of Trust*. These may eventually move into more public-facing forms; this is a discernment held in relationship with the Source.
*Default protection: Member-Open within the ARC, with permissioned outward movement.*

**Contributions under the practice-intelligence proposals** (a proposal, not yet a system). Identifiability is the central variable. Members are offered consent options at the point of contribution — anonymous, pseudonymous, identified, revocable identification — and the contributor's consent is the working final word. Withdrawal of consent is honored.
*Default protection: Consent-determined per contributor; revocable.*

**Member personal information.** Held with structural and covenantal care; never circulated; technical infrastructure (workspace permissions, access logs where appropriate) reflects this.
*Default protection: Source-Intimate by default, structurally enforced.*

### A note on what this taxonomy doesn't yet do

The relationship between the *relational* gradient (Source-Intimate / Holder-Proximate / Member-Open / Public) and the *artifact* gradients (temporal, consent-based, contextual) is not yet fully resolved. Different classes may run on different primary axes — Logos Text is primarily temporal, member art is primarily consent-based, design specs are primarily relational. Whether there is a unifying logic underneath these axes, or whether they remain genuinely plural, is a question the network will continue to live with.

## What is still being clarified

As the network matures, additional clarifications will emerge — whether the artifact gradients reduce to a unifying logic or remain genuinely plural, the specific consent options and revocation mechanisms for the practice-intelligence proposals, the patterns of response for bad-faith violations across different artifact classes, and the eventual role of a dedicated Steward of Information Integrity. The principles above are intended to hold steady; the specifics will be filled in as the network grows into them.

# Our Fundamental Commitments

Three commitments shape our response to [[The Longing|the longing]].

They are not three projects. They are one movement.

## We are here to renew sacred world

Sacred world begins with this Earth: the living body of which we are a part, and through it the larger life in which the Earth participates.

The world is sacred. We have lost the eyes to see it.

To renew sacred world is not to make something sacred that was not. It is to recover contact with the value already here.

You matter. We matter. This matters.

What is difficult to remember alone can become easier to remember together.

## We renew it through intimacy

Intimacy is the capacity to enter real relationship with ourselves, one another, and the world.

When another being becomes real to us, they can no longer be reduced to a role, resource, problem, projection, or instrument of our desire. We become capable of being affected by them without needing to absorb or control them.

The renewal does not happen through the right doctrine held at a distance. It happens as beings learn to meet one another for real, remain distinct, risk truth, receive impact, and allow contact to change them.

Intimacy is the orientation, the method, and the fruit.

There is no substitute for encounter.

## The path is the clarification of desire

Nothing essential needs to be added.

The path is an unburdening. We meet the fear, inherited story, compensatory strategy, unmet need, and borrowed desire that obscure what we most deeply want.

Clarification is not the suppression of desire or obedience to every impulse. It is learning to follow our wanting beneath its strategies, receive it without shame, and let it become answerable to relationship, consequence, and reality.

As desire clarifies, it begins to track toward what is good, true, and beautiful.

## One motion

Renewing sacred world names the direction.

Through intimacy names the way.

Clarifying desire names the path each person walks.

Each depends on the others. Sacredness without intimacy becomes abstraction. Intimacy without clarification becomes fusion or appetite. Clarification without sacred purpose becomes self-optimization.

Together they describe the work.

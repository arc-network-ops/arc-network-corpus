# The Logos Text

The Logos Text is the network's textual ground: an interlinked body of concepts, practices, and orientations that makes the worldview beneath the ARC explicit. It is not doctrine or a substitute for encounter. It is a living articulation, tested and deepened through practice, sourced and stewarded by Daniel in conversation with several lineage streams — the teachings of Rob Burbea, John Churchill's Planetary Dharma, CosmoErotic Humanism, and Aletheia Unfolding.

It lives publicly, in full, at **[publish.obsidian.md/logos-text](https://publish.obsidian.md/logos-text/)**.

The public v1 is a deliberately small launch surface: sixteen pages organized around a threshold essay, the Logos Center (shared ground), optional philosophical depth in the Mesa-View, and two visible areas of living inquiry. Two of its Center nodes — *Our Fundamental Commitments* and *The Design Posture of the ARC* — are companions to the Network Specification and are snapshotted alongside this pointer for completeness. The live site always governs.

# The Practice Intelligence Layer

*A proposal for a two-way passage between the network's lived practice and the Logos Text. The [[ARC Weekly Weave — Design Specification|Weekly Weave]] is the ARC's only live system; what follows is direction, not commitment.*

The Logos Text is a living articulation, tested and deepened through practice. The practice is where the network actually lives. Between them runs a passage that today is carried entirely by memory and teaching: what happens in the dyads shapes what Daniel teaches, and what he teaches shapes the Text.

This proposal asks whether that passage could be widened without being violated. Carefully, consentingly, and in one particular grammar: the gift.

## Two directions of flow

**From the Text toward the practitioner.** A practitioner who has just practiced could receive a small artifact, a *practice mirror*, that places what moved in their session within the larger body the Logos Text holds. Not an analysis. A pointing: here is where what you touched lives in the Text, if you want to go further.

**From the practice toward the Text.** Across many encounters, themes rise and recede. Grief has a season. Trust has a season. If those movements could be sensed in aggregate, with no one exposed, the Source and Practice Holders would have a finer feel for what the field is practicing through, and the Text could grow toward what the network is actually living.

Neither direction is automation of the sacred. Both are ways of letting the network's practice and its language stay in honest conversation.

## The practice mirror

A mirror might contain two things.

A brief thematic sensing, three or four sentences, that names the qualities that moved in the session without quoting, summarizing, or reconstructing what was said. Something like: *this session moved through the edge of allowing yourself to be seen, and a tenderness around trusting someone with what is unfinished.*

And a few entries from the Logos Text, each with a line of context: why this entry might be a companion to what emerged.

That is all. The mirror never evaluates. It does not say the practice went well or badly, does not diagnose, does not advise, does not compare this session to any other. It is not feedback and it is not a record of progress. It is a gift a practitioner may open or ignore, and the practice is complete without it.

Other shapes might be discovered in time. A mirror addressed to the pair rather than the person. Something closer to a poem than a reflection. Whether any of these should exist would be learned from practitioners, not decided in advance.

## Consent, and what is kept

The consent posture is the foundation, and it is strict.

Nothing is recorded by default. A session is only recorded when both practitioners choose it, independently, for that particular encounter. Either may decline without explanation and without the other knowing why. There is no standing opt-in that quietly covers future sessions; each encounter is asked freshly.

When both have said yes, the recording is transcribed, a machine reads the transcript against the Logos Text, and the mirror is generated and delivered to both practitioners. Then the recording and the transcript are deleted. Not archived, not anonymized into a corpus, not kept for training or later analysis. Deleted. What persists is the mirror itself, in the hands of the two people it belongs to.

No human being reads the transcript. Daniel does not see it, stewards do not see it, and no one sees another person's mirror unless its owner chooses to share it. The machine's reading is tool access, not audience access. What happens in the dyad stays in the dyad.

## The field seen whole

From sessions that were consented and mirrored, the system could retain one thin layer: anonymous thematic tags. Grief. Trust. Being seen. Silence. No names, no pairs, no content, no dates fine-grained enough to identify anyone.

Periodically, when there is enough for a pattern and never when there is not, those tags could be gathered into a *field report* for the Source and, as they form, for Practice Holders: the strongest themes of the season, where they touch the existing Text, and the edges where practitioners seem to be reaching for language the Text does not yet hold.

A field report is tending intelligence. It never describes an individual session, never identifies or counts any person, and never recommends what should be taught. It surfaces what is moving; the humans who hold the field interpret it. It is the network becoming faintly perceptible to those who tend it, nothing more.

## How the Text grows

The Logos Text grows the way it has always grown: through Daniel's teaching and stewardship, in conversation with the lineage streams and the life of the network. Nothing in this layer changes that.

What could change is the labor. A tool could draft candidate entries from teaching transcripts and propose links between entries a teaching connected, holding them in a queue for review. Every proposal would pass through Daniel's judgment: approved, reworked, or discarded. The Text never grows by accumulation. It grows by discernment, and the discernment is human.

## The principles that govern

Three principles survived every version of this idea, and any future build answers to them.

**The AI reads — it does not witness.** Processing a transcript for themes is categorically different from being present in the room. No tool in this layer is ever described, designed, or experienced as listening to practice. Presence belongs to the practitioners.

**The practice comes first.** If mirrors ever change how people practice — if sessions become performances for the transcript, if practitioners grow self-conscious before the possibility of a record — the layer is paused or ended. The session is the thing. Nothing downstream of it justifies distorting it.

**The mirror is a gift, not a goal.** No one practices in order to receive a mirror, and no part of the network treats mirrored sessions as more real, more serious, or more valuable than unmirrored ones. Most sessions will never be recorded. That is health, not shortfall.

Beneath all three: this layer keeps no scores, builds no profiles, and produces nothing any person could use to assess another. The deletion of transcripts is not a feature to be traded away in a future version. It is the condition under which the whole idea is tolerable.

## Relationship to the Weave

The Weekly Weave contains none of this and should not grow it quietly. It does not observe, record, or transcribe encounters, and its specification refuses exactly that. If this layer is ever built, it arrives as its own service with its own consent threshold, adjacent to the Weave rather than inside it: the pairing happens as it does now, and the question of a mirror is asked separately, of both people, each time.

That separation is deliberate. A member should be able to live entirely inside the Weave, week after week, and never encounter this layer at all.

## What this awaits

None of this exists, and none of it is urgent. The Weekly Weave carries the network's coordination; teaching carries the Text's growth; both are enough for now.

This proposal exists so that the shape can be felt before anything is committed to. If it becomes the ground for a conversation with someone who might source the building, and if the first practitioners asked about it feel gift rather than surveillance, it will have earned its next step. If not, the passage between practice and Text remains what it has always been: people, speaking to each other, about what is true.

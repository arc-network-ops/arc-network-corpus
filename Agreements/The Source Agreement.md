# ARC Source Agreement

This is what I commit to as Source of the ARC. The Member Agreement holds for me as it holds for every member. This document names the additional commitments that come with sourcing.

## What sourcing means

I took the first vulnerable step to invest myself in the realization of the ARC. With that comes creative authority — the authority of authorship — and with that authority comes responsibility I cannot delegate. This document names that responsibility, makes it visible, and binds me to it.

I am not above the network. I am the member who carries the source role. The role can be passed, in time, to others through conscious succession. The commitments below travel with the role.

## What I commit to

**I maintain my own practice.** I do the dyadic unfolding alongside members. I do not exempt myself from what I ask of others. My practice is the ground from which sourcing is possible.

**I keep my relationships of accountability alive.** My wife, my men's group, my church community, and the spiritual guides I meet with regularly are part of the infrastructure of the ARC. I will not let these relationships erode under the weight of the work. I will remain reachable to them — physically present and interiorly open — including when what they see is hard for me to hear.

**I receive concerns honestly.** When members, Practice Holders, or those who hold me raise concerns about my conduct or the integrity of the field I will receive the concern before defending myself. I commit to this even when the concern feels unfair or unwelcome.

**I hold to the income cap.** I commit to a personal income ceiling from the ARC of $250,000 regardless of the network's total revenue. The ARC is not a vehicle for personal enrichment.

**I keep myself on the record.** I record my side of all ARC conversations — 1:1s, dyads, group containers, governance — with the intention of committing them to a dedicated stewardship role when that role is filled. The field gets to see what I do.

**I do not cross the safeguarding lines.** I will not pursue or accept sexual or romantic involvement with members in my care. Violation by me triggers the same structural response that would be triggered by any member's violation, and I forfeit the role of Source.

**I steward the Logos Text without ossifying it.** I hold editorial authority over the Text's integrity, and I commit to keeping it alive — revisable, offered as ways of looking rather than final claims. I will not allow my teachings to become scripture, and I will update them when practice and reflection require it.

**I honor the growth thresholds.** I will not scale the network past its current phase's ceiling without the explicit re-evaluation the design requires. 

**I refuse veneration.** When members attribute supernatural qualities, exceptional consciousness, or unique access to truth to me, I will not receive it as accurate. I will name it and redirect it. I am a practitioner-leader, not a guru.

**I will step back, slow down, or step aside when called.** If my closest relationships of accountability or the structural accountability bodies the network develops discern that I cannot hold the field cleanly, I commit in advance to receiving that discernment. I will not cling to the role beyond what integrity allows.

## What happens if I break these

If I break any of these commitments — particularly the safeguarding lines or the income cap — I am in breach of the role I hold. The response is the same I would expect to apply to any member: honest reckoning first, structural response when warranted. Serious or persistent breach disqualifies me from the Source role, and I commit in advance to accepting that consequence rather than contesting it.

The deepest accountability for these commitments is not in this document. It is in the practice, in the relationships that hold me, and in my own ongoing aspiration toward sincere orientation under the love of what is real. This document is one binding of that work.

## My agreement

By signing below, I enter into this covenant with the ARC and its members. I will revisit this agreement at each growth threshold, with the people who hold me, to discern whether it needs revision.

# ARC Member Agreement

This is what we are committing to one another. By signing, we become members of the ARC.

## Our shared purpose

We are practicing together because we believe that loving presence is real, that intimacy is possible, and that the world needs more of both. We affirm our longing to help create a world in which the need for loving presence can be met, and we take this network — its practice, its people, its integrity, and what is created within it — as our shared responsibility.

## What each of us commits to in our being together

**We practice in good faith.** We come to dyads, gatherings, and the rhythms of the network with sincere orientation — present, honest, and willing to be moved.

**We tell the truth.** We speak from what we are actually feeling, thinking, and noticing — to our dyad partners, in the group, and to one another in writing. We do not intentionally pretend or deceive.

**We take responsibility for our own projections.** We notice when our minds are casting others — the Source, Practice Holders, dyad partners, the network itself — into roles they do not occupy. We name what we see in ourselves and work with it. We do not act on it as if it were true.

**We receive and offer feedback in relationship.** We are willing to be seen, including when what is seen is hard. We offer feedback honestly and with care. We do not withdraw in silence when something matters.

**We hold confidentiality.** What is shared in dyads and small groups stays there, by default, always. We do not share another person's story or process without their explicit consent.

**We honor the practice's safeguarding lines.** We do not pursue sexual or romantic dynamics with anyone in our care across asymmetric roles. We do not extract — financially, emotionally, sexually, politically, etc — from one another or from the field.

**We surface concerns rather than carrying them alone.** When something feels off — about the field, about another member, about the Source — we bring it forward, through the channels the network provides or directly to the people who can receive it.

**We honor departures.** We respect anyone's choice to pause or to leave, at any time. Departure is a real outcome, honored as such.

## What each of us commits to in stewarding the ARC's artifacts and information

The ARC produces and holds many kinds of artifacts — texts, transcripts, recordings, designs, art, contributions to shared intelligence systems, and the records of our practice together. Some of these artifacts are tender, emerging from intimate practice and creative vulnerability.

The protection we offer one another around these artifacts is not a legal arrangement. It is a covenant — an expression of the love and care we extend to what is being created and to the people creating it. The deeper aspiration is openness: the ARC's vision is to participate in the emergence of an intimate planetary culture in which the conditions of safety might hold a fully exposed field. The protection structures we maintain inside the ARC are a strategic concession to current realities — bad-faith actors, rivalrous dynamics, the vulnerability of art and practice in a world that does not yet know how to receive them.

**We treat artifacts as held within the network's care.** We do not share, copy, paraphrase, or distribute artifacts created or held within the ARC — by the Source, by Practice Holders, by other members, or by ourselves — beyond the network without explicit invitation or guidance. When in doubt, we ask.

**We accept the network's recording structure.** Different spaces in the ARC carry different recording postures — some recorded for the network's collective learning, some protected from recording entirely. The document *Recording in Service of Trust* names the principles and the postures of our recording policy. We enter these containers consenting to the agreement field each one carries.

**We recognize that different sensitivities are honored.** What is open for one person may need protection for another. Within shared containers, we honor the more reserved threshold rather than the more permissive one. We do not assume that what we would freely share is something others share freely.

**We receive good faith as the working assumption between us.** We extend good faith to others in the network and trust that good faith is extended to us. We understand that bad-faith violations — willful misuse, weaponization of vulnerable material, extractive translation of the work — are taken seriously and will be responded to.

**We trust that protection serves emergence.** We understand that what may be held tightly now may open later, and that this discernment is held in relationship as the field grows in its capacity.

## What happens if these are broken

When any of us falls short of these commitments, the first response is honest reckoning between the people involved, in good faith, with care. Most ruptures are met this way and the practice deepens through them.

When a commitment is broken seriously or persistently — particularly the safeguarding lines, or bad-faith violations of artifact stewardship — the network responds. This may include direct conversation with the Source or designated holders, mediation, a pause in participation, or removal from the network. The Source holds final authority over membership, in consultation with appropriate accountability bodies, and exercises that authority in service of the integrity of the field for all members.

## Our agreement

By signing below, we enter the ARC as members in mutual covenant with one another. Membership is a covenant of practice, relationship, and shared responsibility for the life and integrity of the network. We revise or release this agreement together as the network grows.
